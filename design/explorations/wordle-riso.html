01

Screens

One Monday morning, pulled as four sheets. The triad stays exactly where Wordle trained your eye to look for it; everything around it is paper, key ink and two spot colours.

02

System

Five drums and a key plate. Colour fills are always a separate pass with mottled coverage and a small, consistent offset per ink; text and outlines are always the key plate, so legibility never depends on registration.

02.1

Each swatch shows the solid and a 4px halftone tint — the only two ways an ink is ever used. Contrast pairs are computed live from the hex values below.

02.3

Tile states

Same order, same meaning as Wordle. Each state is also printed with a different screen, so it reads without colour.

  • ACorrect — right letter, right spot. Green, solid coverage.
  • RPresent — in the word, wrong spot. Sunflower with a 4px dot screen.
  • EAbsent — not in the word. Slate with a 45° line screen; the letter is knocked out to paper.
QTAD Keys: unused · present · correct · absent

02.6

Motion · the print pass

T A R D Y
80

Flip, strike, settle. Each tile turns on its X axis (460ms, 260ms stagger). At the edge-on frame the colour drum strikes — it lands six times further out of register, then settles to its resting 1–2px offset with a small overshoot. The score prints the same way: pink plate first, key plate dropped on 280ms later.

Reduced motion: no rotation or travel — plates simply appear in place, one tile at a time.

03

Notes

Where the print metaphor earns its keep — and where it should stay out of the way.

03.1

Signature moments

  1. Result reveal

    The score is pulled in two passes: the pink plate slides in, then the key “80” drops on top and never quite lands in register. Guesses count up underneath like a page number.

  2. Share

    “Share result” pulls a small print off the tray — a card with grain and a crop mark slides up; the clipboard gets the emoji version so it still works in any group chat.

  3. Daily rollover

    At 00:00 the countdown hits zero, the control strip wipes across the masthead and the issue number re-prints (No. 278 → 279). Yesterday’s cards drop to a 15% tint, today’s print fresh.

  4. Rank change

    The old rank numeral gets struck through in slate; the new one is stamped on in pink at a 3° tilt, then the score bar re-inks to its new length.

  5. Spoiler wall

    Friends’ rows sit under a dense halftone. When you finish, the dots shrink to nothing row by row — the reveal is the halftone dissolving, not a modal.

03.2

Tweak knobs

  • Misregistration

    0px reads clean and corporate; 3px reads loud zine. Default is 1.3–2.1px per drum (live slider above).

    registeredsloppy
  • Grain & ink mottle

    Coverage texture inside fills plus paper speckle. Dial down for small Android screens.

    flatgritty
  • Drum count

    Strict 3-drum (green / sunflower / slate + key) for maximum Wordle recall, or keep pink + blue for per-game identity.

    3 inks5 inks
  • Type width

    Anybody’s width axis: push display to 50 for posters, pull to 80 if names need to breathe.

    condensedexpanded
  • Paper stock

    Natural, bright white, or kraft for light; black stock for dark.

    naturalkraft
