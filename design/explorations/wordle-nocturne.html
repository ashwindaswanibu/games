    Motion · the turn

    The signature move is Wordle's flip, with one addition: as each stone turns face up, a single sheen runs across it, like light on a watch crystal.

    TARDY
    • Turn640ms · rotateX, eased (.4, 0, .2, 1)
    • Stagger150ms per tile, left to right
    • Face swapExactly edge-on at 90°. The hallmark and girdle arrive with the colour
    • Sheen520ms sweep from the moment of the swap, then gone
    • ReducedHonours prefers-reduced-motion: a 260ms cross-fade with no turn and no sheen

    Notes

    Signature moments · tweak knobs

    3.1

    Signature moments

    1. iResult reveal

      The last tile turns. Then the score dial winds from 0, lighting its indices one at a time (900ms), and the Bodoni "80" lands last. One sheen crosses the crystal. No confetti.

    2. iiShare

      In the app the trace is set like a row of stones. The copied text stays plain so it survives any group chat: Daily Nº 214 · Number Hunt 3/7 · 80 ▼▲◆

    3. iiiDaily rollover

      All day the reserve complication drains toward midnight. At 00:00 the edition number clicks over like a date wheel (Nº 214 → 215) and every card goes back to unlit tiles.

    4. ivRank change

      Rows move like a date wheel: one crisp 240ms step with no bounce. The Roman numeral turns over and the gap readout ("16 pts behind Priya") counts again in mono.

    5. vSpoiler wall

      Friends' results sit behind a frosted caseback stamped with a lock hallmark. You can see colour but never letters. Finishing lifts the frost from left to right with the same sheen as the flip.

    6. viTwo editions

      Night is the default: blue-black and champagne. Day is an opaline silver dial with brushed-gold hairlines. The stones are cut the same way in both, so a share grid reads the same in either.
