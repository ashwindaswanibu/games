03Notes

Both themes are designed: night is the lit cabinet, day is its printed side-art. Neither is a fallback.

Signature moments

  • Result reveal: “Stage clear!” Two-frame degauss flash, the script stamps in, then the score tallies 00 → 80 in pixel digits with coin-counter ticks. The ▼ ▲ ★ marks drop in one at a time like sprites.
  • Share. Copies a three-line cabinet card (DAILY · NUMBER HUNT · 3/7 · 80, with the marks as emoji arrows and a green square; emoji live only in the copied text). The button flips to COPIED and throws an 8-square pixel burst in the game’s accent.
  • Daily rollover: insert coin. The coin-door countdown is the app’s only clock. At 0:00:00 the slot light flares, a coin sprite drops, every card flips from DONE back to PRESS START, and only players who cleared yesterday keep their streak.
  • Rank change. The high-score table re-sorts the way arcade name entry does. Your initials flash three times in the new rank colour, and a ▲1 pip stays beside them for the day.
  • Spoiler wall. Friends’ rows sit behind a locked coin door as a pixel mosaic. Finishing slides the door up and de-pixelates the rows in six steps (block 12 → 1), so the reveal feels earned.
  • Theme switch = power cycle. Going to night powers each screen on CRT-style: a horizontal line that blooms open. Going to day drops the glow for ink outlines and hard offset shadows.

Tweak knobs

  • CRT intensity

    Scanline alpha (0–.25), glow radius (0–16 px), power-on flicker. Turn it up for drama, down for long reading.

  • Pixel-font share

    Right now Press Start 2P covers numbers, tiles and titles. Pull it back to numbers and tiles only if game names start to feel shouty.

  • Wordle-ness of tiles

    Slide from canonical green/yellow/grey to full neon; the pip/hatch cues can be switched off for purists.

  • Sunset chrome

    Banded 8-bit stops vs smooth airbrush. Keep it to the logo, the big score and the primary button.

  • Motion tempo

    Flip 520 ms with a 240 ms stagger. The blinking caret and the INSERT COIN colour cycle can go fully still.
