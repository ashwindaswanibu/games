Every tile flashes fully on, then settles to how true it is: correct stays lit, present drops to half, absent switches off. The score latches up on the segment display in the same stepped cadence. Hard frames and no easing, like a real 1989 LCD. With reduced motion on, tiles and digits jump straight to their final state.

03NotesSignature moments & tweak knobs

Signature moments Day & Night both designed

  1. Result reveal: power-on latch

    The LCD blanks for one frame, then the score counts up on the segment display (00 → 80) with the unlit ghost 8s still visible behind it. The share marks then drop in one per beat.

  2. Share: the A button

    Sharing is a physical press. The output is plain geometric glyphs (▼ ▲ ■), not emoji, so it looks the same in every group chat. The glass flashes inverted for a single frame to confirm.

  3. Daily rollover: LCD self-test

    At 0:00:00 every segment lights (8:88:88), the power LED blinks twice and the new cartridges scan in top to bottom, one row of pixels per frame.

  4. Rank change: hard swap

    Rows trade places in two hard frames with no tween, and the moved row blinks inverted twice. A small ▲ sprite stays beside your rank for the rest of the day.

  5. Spoiler wall: pixels off

    Friends' rows show as unlit checker dither under a padlock sprite. Finishing dissolves the dither in four steps, one row at a time. Nobody gets a spoiler, and the screen never looks empty.

Tweak knobs what to dial

  1. Shell presence full ↔ bezel-only

    The full shell gives the most character. A bezel-only frame keeps the stripes and LED and gives the LCD the whole phone height on small devices.

  2. Palette cartridge pea ↔ pocket grey ↔ amber

    Swap the four LCD tokens and the rest follows. Amber negative works well as a "weekend" or seasonal skin.

  3. LCD realism 0 – 8%

    Controls the strength of the pixel grid, the reflector shadow and the ghost segments. Turn it down for a cleaner, flatter read.

  4. Frame rate 12 fps ↔ 60 fps

    Stepped motion is the signature. Smooth easing makes it softer and more modern.

  5. Accent strategy dither ↔ tint

    Strict four shades with a dither per game, or allow one colour tint per cartridge as a "colour edition" palette swap.
